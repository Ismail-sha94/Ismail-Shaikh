Times follow Muscat (UTC+4). Office hours 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, boss call 5:30 to 6:00 PM. Invoices can be in OMR or USD; the other currency is shown at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. An unpaid invoice turns late the day after its due date. Press Edit on any task, invoice or salary query to change it later, including attaching an invoice PDF. Resolved salary queries move to Salary query history. Minutes of meeting stay on the day of the meeting whatever their status, so you can open any past day from the calendar. Press Chat on any task, invoice, salary query or meeting point to ask Claude for an email or a plan; each item keeps its own chat. Deleted items stay in the Deleted tab until you delete them forever.
